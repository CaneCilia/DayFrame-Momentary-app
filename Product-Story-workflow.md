# DayFrame

DayFrame is a simple visual diary built around one idea: capture one photo every day and slowly turn those moments into the story of your life.

## The Story

A user opens DayFrame for the first time.

The app introduces a simple concept:

"One photo. Every day."

There is no complicated setup. The user creates their profile and reaches the main screen, where today's date is shown with an empty photo frame, The user takes a photo using the camera or selects one from their gallery,Once the photo is saved, today's frame is complete,The app then becomes a daily habit, Tomorrow, the user opens DayFrame again and sees a new empty frame waiting for that day's moment.

Over time, these individual photos become something bigger: a visual timeline of the user's life.

## Main Workflow

### 1. First Launch

User opens the app.
The onboarding explains the core idea:
"Capture one moment every day."
The user continues to the home screen.

### 2. Today's Moment

The home screen focuses on the current day.

It shows:

* Today's date
* A large photo frame
* "Capture today's moment"
* Current photo streak
* Previous day's preview

The primary action is taking today's photo.

### 3. Capture Photo

The user taps the capture button.
They can:

* Take a photo with the camera
* Choose a photo from their gallery

After selecting a photo, they can preview it before saving.
The user confirms the photo,The photo becomes today's memory.

### 4. Completed Day

After saving, the empty frame becomes the day's photo.
The app displays the date and optionally a short caption.

Example:

September 13, 2026
"Coffee and rain."

The user can continue using the app or browse their previous memories.

### 5. Daily Habit

The next day, the app automatically creates a new empty frame, The user receives a reminder:

"What's your moment today?"

The goal is not to take hundreds of photos,The goal is to capture one meaningful moment.

### 6. Timeline

As the user continues taking photos, the Timeline becomes populated.
The timeline displays photos chronologically.

Example:

September 13
September 12
September 11
September 10
September 9

The user can scroll through their life one day at a time.

### 7. Calendar View

The user can switch from the timeline to a calendar,Each completed day contains a small photo thumbnail,cDays without photos remain empty,cThis gives the user an immediate visual overview of their month.

### 8. Monthly Memories

At the end of a month, DayFrame can automatically create a monthly collection.

Example:

September 2026
30 moments captured

The user can browse all of the month's photos together.Later, this can become a monthly recap or short video.

### 9. Yearly Timeline

After several months, the app becomes a visual history of the user's year.

Example:

2026
January → February → March → April → ... → December

Each day contributes one frame.
At the end of the year, the user can look back at the entire year through their photos.

## Core Product Loop

Open DayFrame
↓
See today's empty frame
↓
Capture one moment
↓
Save the photo
↓
Today's frame is completed
↓
Come back tomorrow
↓
Capture another moment
↓
Build the timeline
↓

Look back at your life
This loop is the heart of the application.

## MVP

The first version should stay intentionally simple.

The MVP needs:

* Onboarding
* Home screen
* Camera/gallery photo selection
* One photo per day
* Photo preview
* Save photo
* Daily timeline
* Calendar view
* Photo detail screen
* Basic streak tracking
* Local photo storage
* Daily reminder

The app should work without requiring an account in the first version.

## Future Ideas

Once the core experience works, DayFrame can grow with features such as:

* Captions and notes
* Location attached to memories
* Mood for each day
* Monthly recaps
* Yearly recap videos
* Photo streaks
* "On this day" memories
* Search by date
* Export timeline
* Private cloud backup
* Multiple timeline themes
* Widgets
* Lock screen widgets
* Shareable yearly recaps
* AI-generated life summaries
* Automatic highlight reels

The most important principle is to keep the first version focused.

DayFrame should not feel like another social media app,cIt should feel like a private place where one small moment from every day becomes a record of someone's life.
